REVIEW MEMO — RED FOX SPORTSBOOK FAVORITE INTEGRITY

Branch audit/sportsbook-favorite-integrity, based on production b26c038.
This branch is for review and offline validation. Production policy choices
and any historical classification corrections require separate approval.

DEPENDENCIES AND AUTHORITIES

1. main.py / open registry — DraftKings observed current line, prices,
   tickets/handle and scheduled start; first observed line per sport, event,
   market and side is the registry opener.
2. refresh_anomaly_board._refresh — parses identities and timestamps, selects
   each latest complete synchronized pair, applies completeness, freshness,
   retention and publication windows; expands retained timelines. NFL/CFB
   active-week history uses the seven-day rebase. Cold archive coverage and
   hot input can differ.
3. anomaly_board.build_anomaly_outputs — evaluates path, split, directional
   reaction, held/reversal, evidence roles and Candidate action for each side.
   Action ledger observations can label earlier action on Watch rows.
4. anomaly_board.select_market_leaders / _confirmed_supported_side — one row
   with two side payloads. A sole current Contrarian/Follow or eligible
   counterpart Fade supplies the supported side. Descriptive rank and role
   are distinct from that decision. cross_market_integrity verifies
   synchronized DraftKings spread/ML contradictions; cross_market_split is
   descriptive. Alternate bookmaker context is a separate input.
5. red_fox_favorite.apply_red_fox_favorites — evaluate market, price, side,
   pathway, risk, sport-specific rules and pricing-mismatch vetoes. Evaluate
   eligible Nov 24 extension only after baseline failure. Route expressions
   and apply priority. This output is a raw candidate pending visibility.
6. red_fox_favorite.update_favorite_tracking / _apply_visibility_lock —
   transient review (pending, applied, cleared, late invalidated), candidate
   plus latest tracking and archived row, per-sport intervals and windows,
   recovery, hard overrides. Append tracking and maintain last qualified
   handoff evidence. The public retained-outage overlay runs after this.
7. build_live_recent.main / favorite_handoff_states /
   suppress_stale_retained_favorites — use board, qualified archive, explicit
   tracking and raw paired handoff. Compare source time with kickoff. Dedup
   within a market. Apply historical exclusions and audited corrections.
8. performance_ledger._frozen_records / _attach_results —
   decide official inclusion with version, Supported Side, source-time,
   exclusions and handoff reconciliation. Attach final score/winner and
   grade the captured entry expression. Distribution through
   performance_favorites.csv and favorite_performance.json. The dashboard
   consumes these durable results and a separate live display decision.

Keys in the current graph
  Raw capture: sport/event/market/side/source timestamp.
  Board & score identity: sport/event/market.
  Same-team routing relates multiple markets deliberately.
  Patched pending resolution: sport/event/market + normalized Favorite side
  and rule version + review action, using the uninterrupted matching suffix.
  Entry evidence: side, market, rule version and quote timestamp when stored.
  Outcomes: independently linked provider ID/teams and market settlement.
  Treat provider identity, publication, selection, entry and settlement
  as separate facts during reconciliation.

REPRODUCTION MATRIX (base source at b26c038)

1. Cross-side ordinary confirmation — software defect.
   Source red_fox_favorite.py _review_confirmed, _review_record,
   _apply_visibility_lock. Old condition checks market key and pending action
   plus elapsed time. Intended same wager; actual candidate B can inherit
   candidate A's pending review. CFB and NHL/UFC could also search further
   back than an intervening different pending identity. Repro: set team A
   pending, then identical market with team B as candidate inside the
   applicable interval. The old evaluator can pass. New tests cover both
   actions, all seven sports, aliasing due to changing spread numbers,
   interruptions, changed markets, IDs and versions, and missing old version.
   Affected: board promotion/removal, tracking, handoff, official cohort if
   a different classification survives to kickoff. In copied logs, 551
   adjacent pending/applied addition pairs showed zero changed sides.
   All 1,589 pending rows lack version or timestamp metadata. The original
   use of a non-adjacent pending row cannot be reconstructed reliably.
   Patch: retain side/version/source time; match same contiguous review
   episode. Old pending rows without a version will need to confirm again.
   Existing per-sport elapsed limits remain the same.

2. Board Follow versus Favorite Follow — contradictory live predicates;
   resolution is a policy decision.
   Source anomaly_board.py _evaluate_side / _confirmed_supported_side;
   red_fox_favorite.py _pathway / _matches_confirmed_supported_side.
   Board Follow starts at 70% tickets and 55% money. Nominal Favorite Follow
   requires both splits in 46–60 and an exact Supported Side. With coherent
   splits, the same market's Contrarian/Follow/eligible Fade cannot confer
   that identity. Repro: Stage 2 code_probes.json, 55/55 Watch and 75/80
   Follow at a two-point move. Former passes Favorite pathway helper only;
   latter receives authoritative board support but fails the Favorite
   pathway helper. No archived official row was tagged moderate Follow.
   Signals, qualification and official record would be affected by selecting
   either definition. Both cutoffs and the Tested T−60 public Follow cohort
   remain as originally recorded. No counterfactual selections generated.

3. CFB high handle — contradictory live predicates; policy decision.
   Source anomaly_board.py _evaluate_side and red_fox_favorite.py _pathway,
   _ncaaf_sharp_money_profile and _ncaaf_gate_for_market. Upstream
   Contrarian requires money <=45, downstream high-handle branch requires
   >=46 and Contrarian. Repro: CFB side 30 tickets/60 money, +5 to +3
   and Clean, becomes Watch. A historical official example exists via
   explicit continuity correction. Turning it into a broad Favorite path
   would change policy. The earlier 11–3 is a proxy, never a replay of
   current qualification.

4. November 24 NCAAB ML — improperly inherited/redundant threshold,
   policy choice about intended requirement.
   Source red_fox_favorite.py _nov24_decision / _basketball_moneyline_alignment,
   anomaly_board.py _evaluate_side. The NCAAB caller only tests direction
   and positive movement at its level, but TOWARD already encodes >=2.5 raw
   implied-probability points. Repro: -110 -> -115 is 1.107, LIMITED,
   neutral confirmation; -110 -> -130 is 4.141, TOWARD, confirming.
   Affects NBA/NCAAB alignment, the NCAAB Nov 24 comparison and historical
   interpretation. The any-positive de-vig research proxy is more
   permissive. The copied Nov 24 cohort has no settled grade. Preserve
   deployed gates while the effective floor is explicitly adjudicated.

5. L2 alternate venue identifiers — software defect; activation changes
   the applicability of an existing veto.
   Source refresh_anomaly_board._refresh assigns sport|DK ID,
   l2_scraper.scrape_l2 uses canonical_match.build_canonical_key
   (normalized teams, sport, UTC date), and
   anomaly_board.build_anomaly_outputs filters by exact membership.
   Repro in test_reference_mapping_requires_exact_event_and_refuses_ambiguity
   (all seven sports). The shadow-only exact adapter maps an unambiguous
   reference to DK ID on normalized teams and exact scheduled instant,
   rejects same-matchup collisions, and has no classification consumer.
   Affects cross-book context, Market Lag and therefore Favorite quality.
   Actual historical timely coverage was 30 events and current L2 was
   stale. Do not deploy a new reference match or live fallback until
   feed freshness, schedule tolerances and lag-veto activation are approved.

6. Display, tracking, handoff, ledger — competing authorities;
   part software sequencing issue, part business policy.
   Source refresh_anomaly_board._refresh calls retained restore after
   update_favorite_tracking. build_live_recent.favorite_handoff_states can
   confirm a paired quote despite an explicit falloff, then
   suppress_stale_retained_favorites suppresses it. The ledger has its
   own supported-side and freeze-source gates. Repro: existing Blues
   fixture test_favorite_handoff_keeps_blues_when_final_paired_market_strengthens
   gives true at -125, false after running actual suppression. The final
   audited historical STL decision was separately corrected. A copied
   current snapshot showed no joined difference once applying the proper
   pre-kickoff tracking cutoff. Files copied across workers are not
   transactional. The audit tool reconciles by key and time without
   changing the result. Need approval on whether an explicitly fallen-off
   row may be reinstated by a last quote, and whether an outage badge
   is display-only. Keep both policies exactly as deployed on this branch.

7. Entry versus close sources — software provenance defect.
   Source performance_ledger._tracking_lifecycle, _frozen_records,
   update_performance_ledger and _attach_clv; red_fox_favorite tracking
   used the market leader quote and could carry price across a new side.
   Repro: same game, opposite leader, subject quote -115 vs leader -105;
   frozen side and version with two tracking entries. For close, supply
   close number but only final-pregame price. Patch records the subject
   quote and entry timestamp and separates close-number and close-price
   sources/timestamps. A retained close with absent proof is
   retained_close_unverified. Missing close value keeps the existing
   pregame fallback and is labeled final_pregame_fallback. Existing
   grading and CLV methods unchanged; historic timestamps stay blank where
   unsupported. Impact: four historical entry fallbacks, three different
   quotes in 46 comparable near-close comparisons. No evidence warrants
   changing a grade solely from those three comparisons.

8. Signed American units — discontinuity proven; unit and boundary policy.
   Source red_fox_favorite._material_addition / _material_removal and
   build_live_recent._market_movement_value. Repro: +105 -> -105
   is 210 absolute American units, 2.439 raw implied points. The existing
   15-unit addition gate passes. Same-order direction tests across
   pick'em can keep their sign; magnitude thresholds need explicit
   policy. Affects late addition/removal and study threshold comparisons.
   Old archives cannot isolate all threshold-triggered decisions. Keep
   the 15-unit boundary pending approval of raw versus no-vig units,
   equivalent threshold, and treatment of missing paired prices.

9. Generic falloffs — software auditability defect.
   Source red_fox_favorite.update_favorite_tracking discarded most review
   or cause text; _qualify_market returned only accept/no decision.
   Repro: tracking actual loss-of-status fixture with recorded cause.
   Patch persists review/qualification diagnostic, actual source time
   when available, and the exact side/rule on review records. If multiple
   checks fail, record the first evaluated gate; distinguish absent market
   and unknown root causes. The old disappearance_reason column is
   retained for compatibility. At historical cutoff 12 of 18 stayed-out
   wagers retained only a generic qualification failure. The reason text
   cannot be backfilled with a signal inferred from the outcome.

10. Repeated unchanged observations — persistence is a legitimate business
   rule; independence is an unsupported interpretation.
   Source anomaly_board._build_history_points, _whipsaw_confirmation and
   cross_market_integrity._conflict_streak. Repro: same state at three
   timestamps, with one duplicated timestamp; three retained points, one
   distinct state, zero movement. Archive unchanged adjacent shares:
   NFL 91.1%, NBA 82.1%, NHL 82.4%. Affects any test interpreting captures
   as independent updates, and data-quality gates with counts. Source
   times are persisted on the branch. Require separate policy approval
   before making distinct price changes a gate. Provider sequence and
   actual ticket/handle counts are unavailable.

ADDITIONAL INHERITED/REDUNDANT CONDITIONS TO TRACK

  NBA 2.5-point explicit confirmation repeats upstream TOWARD under
  normal data. A CFB intact partial Whipsaw can set base-quality true
  before its specialized helper checks no-retrace, 80% retention and
  duration; the specialized helper does not recheck every base-quality
  input. Market Read's Hold/Pressure tags are descriptive, while the
  actionable Freeze test has tighter thresholds. Some board snapshots
  select prices from the retained path and latest splits from the admission
  pair, so selected side source timestamps and provided admission time
  should be compared for faithful replay. The old “short home” football
  gate has no home-team condition. The only whole-strategy research record
  is a mixed-version production series with event-level overrides. No
  inference about independent impact of a particular threshold follows.

RELEASE ORDER AND RISKS

  1. Review the proposed identity and provenance migration first.
     Test copied data with full legacy schemas before any approved rollout.
     Risk: a still-pending old row without version restarts confirmation;
     future candidates may wait one more capture after a side or version
     switch. Entry now uses the correct side; past official rows are
     preserved. Future attribution improves; no past timestamp is guessed.
  2. Compare board, tracking, raw handoff and ledger from synchronized
     copies. Keep the audit collector read-only. Decide which state owns
     kickoff classification and which retained badge is display-only
     before consolidating. Risk: parity changes can change what is graded.
  3. Validate the reference identity adapter in shadow on a fresh feed,
     with data rotation, different scheduled times, doubleheaders and
     unambiguous book IDs. Vote on activation of the existing lag veto.
  4. Resolve the Follow, CFB high-handle, NCAAB floor, and American-unit
     measurement policies individually. None of the exploratory winning
     cohorts is an authorization to change a qualification pathway.
