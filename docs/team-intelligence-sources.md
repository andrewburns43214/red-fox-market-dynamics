# Team Intelligence sources and coverage

Team Intelligence reads the existing archive and official Favorite performance ledger. The archive remains the source for quotes, splits, market movement, grading and Favorite entry or closing prices. The separate worker publishes sport snapshots when the market publisher's lock is free. Optional sources use bounded requests, per-source caches and monitored failures.

## NFL schedule and efficiency

Strength of schedule is the mean win percentage of each opponent in the completed regular-season schedule. We use games known before the upcoming fixture in the current source snapshot. The calculation excludes games against the selected team, gives ties half a win and counts repeated opponents once per matchup. The recent view uses the last five opponents. The stronger and weaker opponent splits use the same as-of opponent records, with .500 as the boundary. League rank requires all 32 teams to have a computable measure. Archive-only sports never receive a league strength-of-schedule rank.

EPA per play divides summed passing and rushing EPA by pass attempts, sacks suffered and rushing attempts. Offensive EPA measures the selected team. Defensive EPA allowed uses the opposing team's offense in those same matched games. Samples show their match counts, and league ranks require complete matched game coverage for all 32 teams. These measures are unadjusted for opponent quality. Injury statuses come from game-week reports and expire in the profile after three days. A missing report does not establish a player's availability.

## Market evidence

A historical market grade uses the exact side and priced pregame checkpoint from the archive, settled against the verified score. The popup loads optional observations only when a user expands a row. When a complete paired capture cannot be reconciled to the displayed line, price and observed timestamp, the checkpoint remains visible and the timeline is marked unavailable. Source-reported 0% and 100% splits are labeled as such. Their presence does not prove a data error or establish the underlying distribution of wagers.
